# Research Plan

The study freezes nominal PID, LQR, and LQI gains, then evaluates them on identical actual plants. Experiments cover nominal tracking; isolated thermal-capacity and heat-transfer deviations at -30%, -20%, -10%, 0%, +10%, +20%, and +30%; a -700 W step load; 12 paired noise seeds; restricted actuator limits; and three combined cases. Primary comparisons use IAE and RMSE, while overshoot, settling, steady error, effort, and saturation expose tradeoffs.

Acceptance checks cover physical parameter validity, equilibrium, analytical propagation, DARE residuals, closed-loop poles, augmented controllability, saturation, anti-windup recovery, reproducibility, finite values, and CSV row counts.
