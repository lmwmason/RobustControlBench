# Chemical Background

Temperature measures thermal state, while heat is transferred energy. A solution with larger mass or specific heat requires more joules for the same temperature rise, so its thermal capacity `m cp` is larger and its response is slower. Heat also leaks toward ambient: the farther the solution is from ambient, the larger `UA(T-Ta)` becomes. At thermal equilibrium, incoming actuator power exactly balances that loss.

No reaction kinetics are included. The vessel represents non-reactive mixing, so the control-theory comparison is not complicated by reaction heat, concentration changes, or Arrhenius behavior.
